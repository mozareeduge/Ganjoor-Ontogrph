# Use-Status And Claim Permission

Use-Status and Claim Permission are separate fields. Never merge them into confidence, support count, frequency, or review status.

Use-Status says what a record may currently do: descriptive, heuristic, documentary, artistic, technical, scholarly, residual, blocked.

Claim Permission says how far a proposition may travel: preserve only -> describe locally -> describe distribution under declared conditions -> argue cautiously -> argue -> block.

Rules for the agent:

- AI proposal != evidence. Agent-authored proposal files, candidate labels, lexical neighbors, and dashboards start at preserve only until governed review and source-backed operations change their status.
- Review approval != OccurrenceAssessment. It can promote an Object Address/anchor, but accepted occurrences still require walk or another valid assessment route.
- Provenance != corroboration. A well-recorded source path proves traceability, not truth of an interpretation.
- Finding != artifact. A Markdown/HTML report or release file can preserve a Finding only when the Finding cites governed OperationRecords and source manifests.
- Co-incidence != Relation-Object and conditional association != causation. Do not create relation language from a pair count, high support, lift, or graph position.
- Legacy-unframed operations can be read and cited as limitations or proposal background, but cannot support a Finding, Claim, Relation-Object, DescriptiveCatalog, or verified scholarly release.
- Never assign `argue` or `argue cautiously` on your own initiative. Propose a restrictive permission level and ask the researcher to decide.
- If permission is unclear, choose the more restrictive level and state the missing governed support.
